ArtFreq

by AnyMeans

ArtFreq turns words, pictures and brush strokes into sound. Look at that sound in a spectrogram (an analyser that shows frequencies scrolling by, such as MiniMeters) and you see your drawing. It comes as two plug-ins from the same code, with the same window:

  • ArtFreq, an instrument for MIDI and instrument tracks: it plays the drawing.
  • ArtFreq FX, an audio effect for audio tracks, buses and the master: it carves the drawing into your song, so it shows even in a full mix (or keeps only the drawing, or plays it on top).

This installs both (each can be left out under Customize) in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, for Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig and other VST3 hosts.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 10.15 or later.
