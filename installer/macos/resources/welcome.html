FreqArt

by AnyMeans

FreqArt is an instrument that turns words, pictures and brush strokes into sound. Look at that sound in a spectrogram (an analyser that shows frequencies scrolling by, such as MiniMeters) and you see your drawing.

This installs:

  • FreqArt VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, for Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig and other VST3 hosts.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 10.15 or later.
