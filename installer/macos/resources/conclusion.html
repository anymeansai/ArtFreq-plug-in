FreqArt is installed

First steps:

  1. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if FreqArt (by AnyMeans) does not show up by itself.
  2. Make an instrument (MIDI) track and load FreqArt on it.
  3. Open a spectrogram on its output. In MiniMeters, choose the Spectrogram with a logarithmic scale from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.
  4. Press play in your DAW. The word “FreqArt” scrolls past in the spectrogram.
  5. Now make it yours: type text, drop in a picture or paint with the brush.

Click ? in FreqArt’s window for the full how-to. If your analyser shows frequencies differently, match it in FreqArt’s Settings (the gear).
