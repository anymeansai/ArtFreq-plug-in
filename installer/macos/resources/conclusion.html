FreqArt is installed

First steps:

  1. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if FreqArt and FreqArt FX (by AnyMeans) do not show up by themselves.
  2. To play a drawing, make an instrument (MIDI) track and load FreqArt on it. To draw into a song, load FreqArt FX on an audio track, a bus or the master.
  3. Open a spectrogram after it. In MiniMeters, choose the Spectrogram with a logarithmic scale from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.
  4. Press play in your DAW. The word “FreqArt” scrolls past in the spectrogram: played by FreqArt, or carved out of the song by FreqArt FX.
  5. Now make it yours: type text, drop in a picture or paint with the brush.

Click ? in FreqArt’s window for the full how-to. If your analyser shows frequencies differently, match it in FreqArt’s Settings (the gear).
