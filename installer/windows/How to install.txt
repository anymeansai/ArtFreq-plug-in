ArtFreq and ArtFreq FX for Windows
by AnyMeans

Two VST3 plug-ins for 64-bit DAWs on Windows 10 and 11.

The easy way is the installer, ArtFreq-<version>-Windows.exe, from the same
download page as this zip: run it and it puts both plug-ins in place (and
replaces older copies). The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may say
"Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.

This zip is the manual way: you copy two folders by hand.

1. Close your DAW.

2. Extract the zip first: right-click it, choose Extract All..., then click
   Extract. (Double-clicking a zip only shows what is inside it; nothing can
   be installed from there.)

3. Copy the two extracted folders ArtFreq.vst3 and ArtFreq FX.vst3 into

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   (delete any older ArtFreq.vst3, ArtFreq FX.vst3, FreqArt.vst3 or
   FreqArt FX.vst3 there first). Windows asks for permission to copy there:
   click Continue. Copy each .vst3 folder whole; do not open it or move what
   is inside.

4. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins (in its plug-in settings).
   Ableton Live: Preferences > Plug-ins > Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders: On,
   then Rescan (in Live 12, Preferences is called Settings).
   ArtFreq is listed under instruments: put it on an instrument (MIDI) track.
   ArtFreq FX is listed under audio effects: put it on an audio track, a bus
   or the master.

5. Open the plug-in's window and activate it with the key from your purchase
   email: paste it and click Activate. This is needed once per computer (a
   key works on two); an ArtFreq Pro key activates both plug-ins.

To remove them, delete the two folders from the VST3 folder again.
