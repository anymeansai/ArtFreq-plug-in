ArtFreq and ArtFreq FX for Windows
by AnyMeans

Two VST3 plug-ins for 64-bit DAWs on Windows 10 and 11. There is no installer:
you copy two folders by hand.

1. Close your DAW.

2. Unzip this zip (right-click it, Extract All...), then copy the two folders
   ArtFreq.vst3 and ArtFreq FX.vst3 into

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   Windows asks for permission to copy there: click Continue.
   Copy each .vst3 folder whole; do not open it or move what is inside.

3. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins (in its plug-in settings).
   ArtFreq is listed under instruments: put it on an instrument (MIDI) track.
   ArtFreq FX is listed under audio effects: put it on an audio track, a bus
   or the master.
