# AnyMeans brand files

The AnyMeans mark: an A and M sharing one stem, built like ArtFreq's AF emblem. It is solid on
the left and dissolves into spectral lines on the right.

**Files.**
- `anymeans-mark.svg` is the mark for dark pages, and `anymeans-mark-light.svg` is for light pages. `anymeans-mark-mono.svg` is one colour (`currentColor`, black by default), for print, embossing and coloured backgrounds. All three are vector files with a transparent background and 14 lines.
- `anymeans-lockup-dark.svg` / `.png` and `anymeans-lockup-light.svg` / `.png` put the mark beside "AnyMeans" for the website header, documents and press. The wordmark is outlined, so no font is needed. The PNGs are 1989 px wide and transparent.
- `anymeans-mark-1024.png` / `-512.png` (for dark backgrounds) and `anymeans-mark-light-1024.png` / `-512.png` are the mark alone on a transparent square.
- `anymeans-icon-1024.png` is the mark on a near-black rounded square, for app icons and social avatars. It still fits when a platform crops it to a circle.
- `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png` and `favicon-64.png` are the same tile drawn separately at each size. At 16 px it is solid, without lines. Use them on their own, not scaled down from a larger file.

**Colours.**

| Use | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Ink (letters on light backgrounds, the icon's tile) | `#0b0d12` |
| Letters on dark backgrounds | `#ffffff` |
| Wordmark on dark backgrounds | `#e8ebf2` |
| Lines on dark backgrounds, left to right | white, then yellow `#fce7a0`, orange `#f9a13a`, coral `#e8664a`, magenta `#b83a6e` |
| Lines on light backgrounds | ink, then plum `#7a1f7f`, magenta `#b83a6e`, coral `#e8664a` |

The wordmark is Inter SemiBold with letter spacing −1.2 %.

**Minimum size.**
- The mark: 24 px wide on screen, 10 mm in print. Below that, use the favicon files.
- The lockup: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
- Leave clear space of a quarter of the mark's height all round.

All of these files are generated by `AnyMeansLogoLab brand docs/brand`. The tool is in `tools/AnyMeansLogoLab.cpp`, and the shapes are in `src/brand/AnyMeansMark.cpp`. To get an SVG with more lines for a large print, run `AnyMeansLogoLab mark-svg dark 24 out.svg`.
